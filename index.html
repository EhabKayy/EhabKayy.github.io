Projects

E.H.A.B.

Active · v2 live

Enhanced Human Athletic Biometrics. An AI physical therapy coach that runs in the browser. It tracks 33 body landmarks from a webcam in real time, counts reps, scores your form on every rep, and measures joint range of motion so you can track recovery over time.

  • Per-rep form scoring: catches elbow drift, shallow squats, knees caving in, and rushed tempo
  • Range-of-motion mode for physical therapy, with progress charts over time
  • Runs fully on-device with MediaPipe Pose; no video ever leaves the browser
