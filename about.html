About me

My story

My start in tech began with my hands, doing jobs and tasks such as: running networks, cameras, and access systems across Miami, then as technology evolved I moved into AI work at Outlier AI. What changed the direction of my career was my mother's fight with lung cancer.

Ever since then, I spent a lot of time in the hospital while she underwent her treatment. I watched the scanners, the monitors, and the people who ran them; I saw technology and medicine come together to give her a fighting chance for life, and with this help she beat it. All that time there made me realize and care more about what technology can do for patients and how far it can still go.

That's why I build AI for health tech. I want to make tools that help people recover, move, and live better. If my work helps save even one life the way those systems helped save my mother's, then the work will be worth it.
